# Brand

## Name

**Overtime.** Work that keeps going after you stop. Say "Overtime AI" in titles where the word alone is ambiguous.

## Logo

<img src="assets/logo.png" alt="Overtime wordmark with a small punch clock" width="300">

A heavy grotesk wordmark with a small punch-clock icon beside it. The icon alone is the app icon. Concept art for now; to be redrawn as vector.

## Colour

Time-clock green with safety yellow. Green means selected and fine; yellow means it needs you. Black and white do the rest.

| Role | Light | Dark |
|---|---|---|
| Ink | `#111111` | `#ECEDEA` |
| Paper | `#ECEDEA` | `#141A17` |
| Machine green | `#7E9C8C` | `#5F7A6C` |
| Safety yellow | `#FFD21F` (UI: `#E9C46A`) | `#E2BC57` |

Rules:
- Yellow only ever sits behind black text, never as text.
- In the app, about three yellow things per screen at most: the selected worker, anything that needs you, the primary button.
- Red and green only for code diffs.

## Type

- **Display:** a heavy, tight grotesk (Archivo at 900 works for drafts).
- **Labels:** a monospace (IBM Plex Mono) for IDs, times and stamps: `AGENT_02 / GROWTH`, `IN 06:40`.
- **App UI:** the system font. The brand stays in the chrome, cards and stamps, not in chat text.

## Motifs

- **The time card** is a worker: ID, role, its live desk, its shifts, one status stamp.
- **The punch clock** is the app: it prints the cards.
- **Stamps** are status: On the clock, Needs you, Off the clock, Stopped, Approved.

## Voice

Plain, confident, a little dry. Short sentences. Office and shop-floor words over AI jargon. No hype, no "revolutionise".
