# Brand

## Name

**Overtime.** Agents that keep working after you stop. Say "Overtime AI" where the word alone is ambiguous.

## Logo

<img src="assets/logo.png" alt="Overtime wordmark with a small punch clock" width="300">

Heavy grotesk wordmark, small punch-clock icon. Concept art; to be redrawn as vector.

## Colour: Signal, cooled

| Role | Hex |
|---|---|
| Paper | `#F6F6F3` |
| Ink | `#0D0D0D` |
| Signal red | `#E3242B` |
| Cobalt | `#2741D6` |

- Red for machines and actions. Cobalt for status stamps and grids.
- Neutral white, never cream: keeps us clear of Claude's look.

## Type

- Display: heavy, tight grotesk.
- Labels: monospace for IDs, times and stamps (`AGENT_02`, `IN 06:40`).

## Motifs

- **Time card:** one agent.
- **Punch clock:** the app.
- **Stamps:** status (On the clock, Needs you, Off the clock, Stopped).
