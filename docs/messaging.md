# Messaging

Working document for how we talk about Overtime. Change it freely; the README and site follow it.

## Positioning

**For** founders and small teams who want AI to do real work in their real accounts,
**Overtime is** a Mac app and server kit for running a team of AI workers,
**that** gives every worker its own computer and browser you can watch and take over,
**unlike** agent frameworks and coding UIs, which run headless in a sandbox or only write code.

## The line

**Headline:** AI workers that never clock out.
**Subline:** Each one gets its own computer and browser. Watch them work, take over anytime.

Alternates to test:
- Run a hive of AI workers. They work while you sleep.
- Hire AI workers. Give them a desk.
- Your AI team, on the clock while you sleep.
- AI employees for your Mac.

## Three things to say, in order

1. **They do real work.** A desk per worker: real apps, a real browser, your logins. Ads, content, leads, books.
2. **You stay in charge.** Watch any desk live, take over in a click, approve anything with consequences.
3. **It is yours.** Your server, your Claude Code and Codex logins, separate workspaces for company and personal.

## Proof we can show

- A worker's desk streaming live, and a take over that passes a 2FA prompt.
- A real approval: "Growth needs you: launch spring campaign, $40/day?" with the filled form.
- The weekly timesheet with numbers pulled from connected sources (Stripe, ad platforms), never typed in.
- A worker answering the same request on Telegram and in the app.

## Distribution

The moat is distribution, so every feature should produce something people want to post.

- **The timesheet.** "My workers did this while I slept": revenue, ads launched, videos made, hours of overtime. Only numbers from connected sources, so every screenshot is real.
- **The desk.** A screen recording of a worker using a real app is the demo. Short clips for X and TikTok.
- **Watch my workers.** A read-only public link to your workers' desks, live, with sensitive details hidden. Nobody else can stream the work as it happens.
- **GitHub.** Name and topics for search: `claude-code`, `codex`, `ai-employees`, `ai-agents`, `computer-use`, `browser-use`, `macos-app`, `self-hosted`, `openclaw`. "Claude Code Mac app" has few competing repos.

## Words

| Use | Avoid | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Workers | Agents (in headlines) | People hire workers; agents is jargon |
| Desk | Sandbox, VM | A desk is a place; a sandbox is a test |
| On the clock, Needs you | Running, pending | Human, and on brand |
| Take over | Intervene, override | Plain |
| Your server | Self-hosted (in headlines) | Benefit before category |
| Timesheet | Report, analytics | It is a document people already understand |

Never: "zero-human", "replace your team", "fully autonomous". We sell supervised workers, not replacement.

## Against the alternatives

| They say | We say |
|---|---|
| Paperclip: an org chart of agents in a web dashboard | Workers with desks you can see into, in a native Mac app |
| OpenClaw and assistants: one chat assistant on your messaging apps | A team, each with a role and a computer, on Telegram and the Mac |
| Claude Code and Codex UIs: a better window onto a coding agent | Workers for the whole business, not just the codebase |

## Honesty rules

- Say "next" for anything not built yet. Today a workspace shares one browser; a full computer per worker is next.
- No invented numbers. Example timesheets are labelled as examples.
- No "made me $X" unless it comes from a connected source.

## Open questions

- Launch before or after a full computer per worker? The subline promises it.
- Headline: "never clock out" (work ethic) or "while you sleep" (benefit)?
- Price and model: free and open source with a hosted option, or paid app?
