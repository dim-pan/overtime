# Messaging

## One line

The command center for always-on AI agents doing real work.
An open-source OpenAI dots and Grok Bot alternative. See every agent and every action, Codex-style. Scale from one agent to 100 with ready-made templates.

## The leap

From personal assistants to AI employees: agents you can manage, watch and measure, doing jobs that make money.

## Value

1. **Always-on employees:** an OpenClaw-style harness on top of Claude Code and Codex; OpenClaw and Hermes support coming.
2. **Set up in a click:** a workspace and browser per employee, on your VMs.
3. **The Codex interface:** see every step, diff and browser; take over anytime.
4. **Real work:** browser use and integrations (ads, payments, email, CRM, socials) in the tools businesses run on.
5. **Scale:** 100 always-on employees from one app.

## Our edge

Everyone now has always-on agents with their own computer and browser (OpenAI dots, Grok Bot, Rakazo, OpenMausBot, OpenDots). They are all chat apps for a few assistants. Overtime is different in three ways:

1. **A work view, not a chat:** steps, diffs, files and live screens, like Codex.
2. **Built for fleets:** 100 agents, isolated workspaces, on your own servers.
3. **Native and fast:** a Swift app for Mac and iOS.

## Problem

- **Interfaces suck.** Telegram and chat threads give you a summary, not the steps, diffs and screens behind the work.
- **Computer use doesn't scale.** It only runs on your laptop, one agent at a time.
- **Agents get lost.** Run several from one thread and they talk over each other, with no way to see who is doing what.
- **Hosting is hard.** Running agents at scale in the cloud means wiring up servers, logins and browsers, and keeping them alive yourself.

## Solution

Move the agents to the cloud, keep the app: always-on agents on your VMs, a computer each, one app to watch, steer and take over all of them.

## Thesis

The solopreneurs and businesses that win will be the ones that figure out how to run tens to hundreds of always-on AI agents: doing productive work, coordinating with each other, and knowing when to hand off to a human.
